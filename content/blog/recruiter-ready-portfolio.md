Portfolio structure is now organized into high-level updates in blog posts and technical deep dives in project pages.

The goal is to give visitors two complementary views: quick narrative checkpoints here in the Updates timeline, and self-contained technical case studies under Projects. Each update post captures a moment in time—what was just shipped, what the next experiment is, and why it matters—without requiring a full deep-dive context.

This separation keeps the homepage and project pages focused while letting the story of ongoing work live in one scannable place. Future updates will link back to the relevant project deep dives when the technical detail lives there.
