About

Controls, Simulation, and Systems Work

I build engineering projects where software, physics, and hardware constraints meet, then document the decisions clearly enough for others to inspect and reuse.

My work centers on control systems, simulation tooling, estimation, embedded workflows, and practical validation. I tend to use personal projects as testbeds for the full loop: model the system, build the tooling, run the experiment, review the data, and tighten the design.

This portfolio is a working archive of those efforts. The project pages are meant to show the engineering tradeoffs behind each build, not just the final screenshot.

Focus Areas

  • Controls and vehicle dynamics
  • Simulation and validation tooling
  • State estimation and filtering
  • Embedded systems workflow

How I Work

I prefer tight feedback loops, instrumented tests, and implementation notes that make assumptions explicit. When a result depends on a model, log, or script, I want the source of truth to be easy to find.

What I Am Building Toward

I am interested in work that connects rigorous simulation with real hardware behavior, especially where control logic, autonomy, or engineering tools need to survive messy physical constraints.
